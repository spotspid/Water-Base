# Water Base, page by page

Plain guides to what each page does, what it refuses to do and why, and what
happens after you press the button. Written for the people running the
business, not for developers.

Each guide is walked through against the live app, so the messages quoted are
the words you actually see on screen.

## Guides

- [Quotes](quotes.md): prices sent to customers that nobody has signed yet.
- [Jobs](jobs.md): sales, from written up to installed.

More pages will be added here as they are walked.

## How to read these

- **"Blocked"** means the app will not let you do something. Every block in
  these guides is quoted word for word, so you can search for the message you
  are looking at.
- **"Worth knowing"** is behaviour that is correct but surprising.
- **Anything listed under "Rough edges"** is a fault or an oddity found while
  writing the guide. Those are not instructions, they are a list of things to
  fix or decide about.

## The short version of the whole app

A job moves through five states:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Quoted | A price the customer has not agreed to. Promises nothing. |
| Sold | They agreed. No date yet. |
| Scheduled | It has a date, and its parts are promised for that day. |
| Installed | The work happened. Parts have left the shelf and the money counts. |
| Cancelled | Off the books. Parts it promised are released. |

Money only counts as revenue once a job is installed. Parts only leave the
shelf when a job is marked installed.
