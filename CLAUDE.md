# Working in this repo

## Name the session, every turn

Rename the session at the **start of every turn** and again at the **end**,
using the `set_session_title` tool on `self`. Never skip it, including on
one-line replies.

    🔵 WORKING · 💦 Water Base — <topic>

and at the end, whichever is true:

    ✅ DONE · 💦 Water Base — <topic>          the work is finished
    🟡 YOUR TURN · 💦 Water Base — <topic>     waiting on Steve for an answer or a decision
    🔴 STUCK · 💦 Water Base — <topic>         something is blocked

Three rules about the text, each learned by getting it wrong:

**Keep the 💦.** The project emoji is the fastest thing to pick out of a
sidebar holding several sessions, so the status marker goes in front of it and
never instead of it.

**Name the project.** A title has to say which project it belongs to before it
says anything else.

**The topic is the work in progress**, not a list of what is outstanding.
"backlog of questions" told Steve neither what the session was for nor which
project it was. Pending asks belong in the reply, not the title.

Steve moves between a desktop and a laptop several times a day and runs
sessions side by side. The title is the only part of a session visible without
opening it, so it has to say whether that session needs him, is still running,
or is done. `🟡 YOUR TURN` is the one that matters most: if a turn ends with a
question, an approval, or something only he can do, the title says so rather
than `✅ DONE`.
